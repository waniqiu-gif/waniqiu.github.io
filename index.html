裘凡笑Fanxiao Wani Qiu

I am a tenure-track assistant professor at Fudan University. My research focuses on children's social-cognitive development and the cognitive processes that underlie teaching and learning.

我现任复旦大学长聘轨助理教授。我的研究关注儿童的社会认知发展，以及教学与学习背后的认知过程。

I study how children learn efficiently from limited information, how teaching shapes learning and innovation, and what principles guide information transmission.

我研究儿童如何从有限信息中高效学习、教学如何影响学习与创新，以及信息传递遵循哪些原则。

More recently, I have become interested in the intersections between human and machine learning, specifically computational models of human teaching and human-ai interaction. I combine behavioral experiments and computational approaches to understand the mechanisms behind learning and teaching.

近期，我开始关注人类学习与机器学习的交叉领域，特别是人类教学的计算模型与人机互动。我结合行为实验与计算方法，探索学习与教学背后的机制。

Questions & approaches研究问题与方法

Research研究方向

I study how children acquire, select, and transmit knowledge, with the broader aim of understanding learning and informing educational practices that support motivation and innovation.我研究儿童如何获取、选择与传递知识，希望借此理解学习过程，并为支持学习动机与创新的教育实践提供依据。

01

Teaching and information transmission教学与信息传递

What makes information worth passing on? I investigate how children decide what to teach, how they adapt information to a learner, and how people select examples to communicate a category. I use computational modeling to help test the principles behind these choices.

什么样的信息值得传递？我研究儿童如何决定教什么、如何根据学习者调整教学内容，以及人们如何选择样例来传递类别知识。我借助计算建模检验这些选择背后的原则。

Selective teaching · Pedagogical reasoning · Exemplar selection选择性教学 · 教学推理 · 样例选择

02

Pedagogy and learning教学与学习

How can teaching support discovery and innovation? I examine how different forms of instruction, such as principles, examples, and pedagogical questions, shape children’s learning and their ability to find new solutions.

教学如何支持发现与创新？我考察不同的教学方式，例如原理讲解、具体示例和教学性提问，如何影响儿童的学习，以及他们寻找新解决方案的能力。

Instruction · Innovation · Children’s evaluations of teaching教学方式 · 创新 · 儿童对教学的评价

03

Children and AI儿童与人工智能

How do children understand, interact with, and learn from AI assistants? I study how children choose between human and technological helpers, and how the topic of a question shapes whom they ask for help.

儿童如何理解人工智能助手、与其互动并向其学习？我研究儿童如何在人类与技术智能体之间选择求助对象，以及问题所属的领域如何影响他们向谁求助。

Help-seeking · Social learning · Human–AI interaction求助行为 · 社会学习 · 人机互动

04

Learning from limited information从有限信息中学习

How do children build rich category knowledge from just a few labeled examples? I study how learners combine labeled and unlabeled information, how category representations change with experience, and how children’s learning compares with neural network models.

儿童如何仅凭少量带标签的样例形成丰富的类别知识？我研究学习者如何整合有标签与无标签的信息、类别表征如何随着经验而变化，以及儿童与神经网络模型的学习过程有何异同。

Semi-supervised learning · Categorization · Human–AI comparisons半监督学习 · 类别学习 · 人类与人工智能的比较

Methods研究方法

My work brings together developmental behavioral experiments, meta-analysis, and computational modeling. These approaches connect children’s choices and learning outcomes with explicit accounts of the information they use.

我的研究结合儿童发展行为实验、元分析与计算建模，将儿童的选择和学习表现与其所利用的信息及潜在机制联系起来。
